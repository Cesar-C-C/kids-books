给家长的话 / For grown-ups

牙齿城堡、酸雨警报和橙色小滴是帮助孩子理解蛀牙过程的故事表达。口腔场景经过放大与简化，酸是细菌产生的物质。早期矿物质流失与已经形成的蛀洞不同；出现蛀洞或牙痛时，应由牙医检查处理。

Tooth City, its acid alarm and orange drops are story devices for exploring decay. The scenes are enlarged and simplified; acid is a substance made by bacteria. Early mineral loss differs from an established cavity, which needs dental care.
