眼睛结构和聚焦互动经过简化，不按真实比例呈现，用于亲子探索光线与视网膜的关系，不能用于视力检测或诊断。如孩子看不清或眼睛不舒服，请寻求眼科专业人员帮助。

The eye and focus activity are simplified and not to scale. Explore light and the retina together; the activity cannot test vision or diagnose a condition. Seek professional eye care if a child has blurry vision or discomfort.
