## Review

All seven final illustrations were inspected inline at generation output. Character clothing, face markings and book binding are consistent. Experiment lamp-to-ball path unobstructed, bright hemisphere faces lamp. Full has a plain circle rather than a sun, and corrected grandma has no ray/star symbols or unwanted Moon in the window. Mobile album screenshot verifies paper moon overlays remain inside both papers; labels contrast against the paper. Desktop lab screenshot verifies text and controls separate from illustration. These are simulated browser views, not physical-device checks.

Repeated art is intentional within one story beat; adjacent narration sharing a picture is grouped into one visual panel, not repeated as multiple full copies. Four first-act cards are manually turned; later chapters scroll.
