# Cloud illustration prompts

Built-in ImageGen; cover used as the common character/style anchor. Full-bleed 1516×1038 landscape. Refined gouache storybook illustration, teal/mint/butter-yellow, consistent turquoise droplet and green frog. No printed labels, text, watermarks or baked-in interactive markers. Vapor is invisible; magnified liquid cloud droplets are spherical. All images were visually reviewed.

## 00-cover

Tiny turquoise drop with two dark eyes and rosy cheeks on a lily pad, green frog nearby, magnificent white cloud over mountain valley, winding stream and red-roof cottage, golden light, sense of adventure. No clothing or accessories.

## 01-promise

Intimate ground-level view at the same pond on a later dry afternoon. The SAME turquoise droplet and green frog look worried at a visibly lower pond waterline; exposed muddy bank and a young drooping willow sapling. Cloud reflected in remaining water; no drought disaster. Droplet on lily pad foreground, frog beside it. Narrative: a promise to bring news of rain. No clothes.

## 02-evaporation

Scientific story scene of sun-warmed shallow pond. Warm sunlight on water, frog looks toward clear EMPTY air above shrinking puddle. Crucial: water VAPOR IS INVISIBLE, do NOT draw ascending droplets, mist, steam, smoke, white vapor or a flying droplet. Just bright clear air over warm water and the frog searching. A few ripples and reeds, side-view scene. Character droplet now invisible, deliberately absent.

## 03-condensation

A whimsical magnified view INSIDE A CLOUD: many tiny spherical transparent blue water beads suspended amid soft diffuse white haze, each liquid bead built around a tiny dust speck. One bead has our simple two dark eyes and rosy cheeks to recognize the story protagonist, ROUND now rather than pointy. Big and little spheres, nobody standing on a solid cloud floor. This is magnified storytelling, no molecular atom models. Soft cool pearly mint palette, deep atmospheric space, no ground.

## 04-floating

Looking sideways through a translucent small white cloud above distant forest. Countless tiny ROUND droplets dispersed throughout cloud, one enlarged round turquoise bead with same simple face as reference for story emphasis. Open gaps of sky among particles, wispy moving air implied by soft curved brush marks not arrows; NOT a solid platform, nobody sitting on cloud. Air currents gently lift the tiny beads. Clear educational cutaway-like scene, no labels.

## 05-wind

A spectacular bird's-eye view following white clouds across patchwork farmland toward distant mountains; below, a small red roof cottage and a windsock or fluttering flag clearly leaning same direction as drifting clouds. One tiny round turquoise face bead nestled subtly within nearest cloud. Sense of movement and distance from home, birds flying, bright sky. No solid cloud vehicle, no buildings on clouds.

## 06-mountain

Wide SIDE CROSS SECTION LANDSCAPE of moist air being lifted along the LEFT slope of a green mountain, diffuse cloud thickens over upper left slope and crest. Left foothill is moist green forest, right lee valley sunnier and somewhat drier. Gentle landscape shapes with distant peaks. Cloud a mass of tiny droplets no face no solid platform. No arrows or labels, will add overlays in software. Same illustrated story world, single mountain big central-right.

## 07-dark-cloud

Dramatic but comforting view from meadow looking up at a tall thick cloud. Its top in warm brilliant sunlight, its underside cool blue-gray in shade. Sun peeks at upper left cloud rim, no angry face, no dirty smoke, no lightning. Small cottage in meadow below, flower closes slightly, visual mystery: why cloud bottom dark? Painterly children's book continuity.

## 08-growing-drops

Magnified scientific story view inside cool cloud: many tiny spherical droplets and a few larger ROUND droplets touching and merging; a main bigger round turquoise droplet with our face softly surprised, surrounded by smaller blue round beads. One pair of spheres visibly forming a joining bridge; subtle bottom-oriented motion. NO pear-shaped raindrops, NO cloud floor, no rain bags. Watery reflective surfaces, soft mist-blue background, clear compositional hierarchy.

## 09-rain

Emotional rain scene from near ground looking up past a drooping young willow beside a rural stream. Numerous small ROUND rain drops fall naturally downward; one slightly larger round turquoise character bead with same eyes and cheeks close to a leaf. Gentle rain from gray-blue cloud above, ground absorbs rain, leaf catches drop, splashes in stream. No umbrella on droplet, no lightning, no sun rainbow yet. Warm hopeful mood, child-friendly.

## 10-soil

Beautiful educational cutaway landscape: upper third green grass and young willow with branching roots; lower two thirds brown soil with clearly visible fine pores, sand grains and rock cracks, little blue water traces passing down among soil grains, some water alongside fine roots. Tiny turquoise droplet character rounds in a pore near center. A small worm, not giant underground lakes or tunnels full of free water. River bank at far right. Water held in soil spaces, scientifically plausible, NO labels arrows or text.

## 11-leaf

Educational close-up cutaway of a willow leaf and stem attached to young tree. Green leaf dominates right, gentle veins carry liquid water. Magnified opening on leaf underside shows two green guard cells surrounding a small pore (stoma). Sky beyond is clear because water vapor invisible: NO visible rising steam or water drops outside leaf. Tiny turquoise round character cameo within leaf vein. A bird distant. Consistent warm delicate gouache story illustration, no labels arrows or text.

## 12-home

Happy reunion at the SAME pond as reference, later after rain at sunset. SAME turquoise teardrop character rests on lily pad next to SAME green frog, both pleased, young willow growing at bank, pond refilled by small stream flowing in from mountain valley. Clouds broken apart, warm pink-orange sky and reflected light. Peaceful earned ending, subtle water ripples, no rainbow required. No accessories or writing.

## 13-map

A beautiful panoramic oblique cutaway water-cycle landscape for an illustrated children's book: sea left foreground, winding river from mountain at upper right into sea, pond midground, forest and tiny red roof cottage, white clouds over mountain, local rain beneath one cloud, sun upper left. Shallow cutaway strip lower edge shows soil and roots with water among pores. Lots of legible uncluttered space for code overlays. No giant droplet character; no arrows labels text borders. Same warm painterly palette.

## 14-cup

Cozy child's tabletop observation scene by window. Two transparent unbreakable cups on tray, LEFT holds cold water and ice cubes with small water beads clearly on OUTSIDE wall, RIGHT holds room-temperature water with dry outside wall. Folded cloth beside tray. Outside window our familiar pond and clouds, no people necessary, no hot water or steam. Warm scientific observation illustration, NO labels letters arrows or text.
